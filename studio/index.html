How the work gets done.

  1. Understand

    Clarify the goal, audience, content, and practical requirements before decisions become expensive.

  2. Shape

    Develop the visual direction, structure, hierarchy, and interactions that give the work its purpose.

  3. Build

    Turn the direction into a responsive, functional digital experience with attention to detail and performance.

  4. Refine

    Review the result, remove friction, and polish the details that make the work feel finished.

AI tools can help with ideas, code, and repetitive work. The design decisions, functional testing, responsive behavior, and final review stay under deliberate human direction.
